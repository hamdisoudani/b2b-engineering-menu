What are the exact legal mechanics of the 0% VAT invoice?
For EU/French companies, cross-border B2B digital services are subject to the Reverse Charge mechanism (Art. 259-1 of the French CGI and EU VAT Directive 2006/112/EC). VAT is self-liquidated by the recipient entity. Furthermore, under Article 7 of the Franco-Tunisian bilateral treaty, there is zero French withholding tax (retenue à la source). You receive a clean, compliant invoice payable net via SEPA wire.
